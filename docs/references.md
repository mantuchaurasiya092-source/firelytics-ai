## Repository scope

NASA PSI lists 24 combustion-science investigations with roughly 12.4 TB of aggregate telemetry, imaging and documents. FIRELYTICS deeply indexes the 14 records in the table above and accounts for 10 associated studies (SOFIE, SAFFIRE-IV/V/VI, ACME CLD Flame, ACME s-Flame, FLEX-2J, FLEX-IT, FSDC, MIST, LSP and CFE/ICF) in its scope matrix. LUCI and FM2 are upcoming NASA partial-gravity missions; no LUCI/FM2 data are claimed.

## Partial gravity context

Liao et al., “Challenges and Research Needs for Micro- and Partial-Gravity Fires,” NASA-hosted PDF: [source](https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/60_afb22b1e538432728848e83bb3ade8c2_LiaoYaTingT.pdf).
