Square-wave model predicts an exception during a width-8-to-13 phase window in each period 18–26; all offsets are searched. It maximizes training accuracy with deterministic ties favoring smaller period, then width, then offset. The constant-rate baseline probability is the training exception fraction; thresholding that rate at 0.5 yields the reported majority-class baseline (ties choose agreement). Full-sequence fits are in-sample; holdout results freeze all fit parameters. Precision/recall/F1 refer to exception as positive. Sample SD is reported for block lengths and intervals.
